## 2. What data we process

Depending on how you use Spotter, we process:

- **Account data**: email address and password, or the identifier Apple or Google
  gives us if you sign in with them.
- **Profile data**: name, date of birth, photo, the sports you practise and your
  level in each, your usual training hours and your gym.
- **Approximate location**: the area where you train, so we can show you people
  nearby. We do not keep a history of your movements.
- **Messages**: the content of the conversations you have in the app.
- **Technical data**: device model, system version and crash reports, so we can
  fix problems.
